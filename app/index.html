Roadmap to the Platinum: Vice Got a Price!

55 trophies: 1 Platinum, 2 Gold, 6 Silver, 46 Bronze. All 55 are single-player and offline; there are no online trophies. The order below is built from the official descriptions: story chapters and missions first, then stranger strands, then activities and crime, then the long collectible grinds, and 100% completion last. Only one trophy is possibly missable, and it is flagged in Phase 1.

Missable trophies: 1 possible

Positive Mental Attitude (Bronze, meditate at every spot across Leonida) is the only possibly missable trophy. PowerPyx and KeenGamer report that a Chapter 1 meditation with Lucia at Jason's house may count toward the total; GTADB says the status is unresolved and advises a manual save before leaving Chapter 1. So: meditate at Jason's house while still in Chapter 1, and keep a manual save from before you leave Chapter 1. No other trophy is flagged missable by any source read for this guide.

  1. Phase 1 — Play the story, Chapter 1 to the final mission

    Story trophies unlock automatically as you play; none of them can be missed except the one flagged above. In order: Just Like Paradise (Gone Fishing), Paradise Lost (Chapter 1), Trust (Breakfast Run), Tits Ass Drug Money (Chapter 2), Break the Bank (The Cartel Heist), Family Matters (Child's Play), Vice City, Baby! (Chapter 3), DWNFALL (Headliners), Hi-Jacked (VCIA Takedown), Top Of The World, Ma (Chapter 4), Snow Storm (Operation Savage Glory), then You Gotta Know When to Get Off (Gold, the final mission). Do the Chapter 1 meditation for Positive Mental Attitude before you leave Chapter 1.

  2. Phase 2 — Stranger strands

    Finish the five named stranger chains to their final missions: Road to Rehabilitation (Andy), This Was No Boat Accident (Kenton), The Lone Rider (Billy), Supply & Demand (Randy), Spin On This! (Tyrone). Then find three more stranger strands and finish those too for Strangers & Freaks (Silver, 8 strands). Hunt a unique animal for Bret along the way for Fair Game.

  3. Phase 3 — Activities and skills

    Sample one of every activity type for Master of None. Then work the skill trophies: The Shootist (Silver, gold in all Shooting Range challenges), Top Fun (drone race win at every location), Earn Cash, Not Kudos (20 different race wins), Baited My Tackle (beat Cal fishing), Stick Fiddler (finish an arcade game), Let's Go Bowling (Kolt & Associates bowling alley), Gunslinger (5 takedowns in one Combat Focus), Catch Me If You Can (unscheduled departure at VCIA), One Last Thing (50 challenges), and the partner trophies Hot Together (lose a 4-star wanted level together) and Retail Therapy (3 shops back-to-back while wanted). Meditate at every spot for Positive Mental Attitude and finish Cunning Stunts (Silver, 250 stunts) as you travel.

  4. Phase 4 — Crime and money

    Robberies, in order of difficulty: A Classic Blitz Play (armored truck), Sneakthief (jewelry store, undetected), Maximum Withdrawal (Silver, 5 bank vaults), Pest Control (one illegal operation per gang). Bank the proceeds: Dime on the Dollar ($250,000 laundered Marked Bills) and Treat Yo Self ($100,000 spent across all shops) build up alongside the robberies.

  5. Phase 5 — Collectibles grind

    The long ones, best started early and finished now: You WAiNKer! (Silver, WAiNK It at 100 locations), Don't Touch My Deck (Silver, 3 card sets), Whip It Good (100 vehicles discovered), The Goodtime State (explore all of Leonida), Little Boxes (all garage properties), On the Spot (10 vehicles on the Scooter Bros app).

  6. Phase 6 — Clean-up and 100%

    Five trophies have no confirmed unlock method yet (see their entries: In The Beginning..., Capital Offense, Bad Luck, I Guess, The Truth Is Out There, Ten Pin); check their sources for updates, then clear whatever the in-game stats say is left for A Long Road (Gold, 100% Completion). When that pops, Vice Got a Price! (Platinum) follows.
